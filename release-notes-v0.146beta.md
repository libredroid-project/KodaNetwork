### Full notes

**KodaDash**
- KodaDash access is now a flag on the account (`kodadash_beta`), returned by the profile RPC. A tester can be enabled without building a custom APK.
- The KodaDash row on Paper and Purpur servers only appears for enabled accounts.

**New server**
- The side-view server type dial is the default. The developer option still switches back to the top view.

**Support tickets**
- The ticket form uses the quiet surface, muted labels and one filled action like the rest of the app.
- Questions and ideas hide the whole subject section. No empty label is left behind, and the first line of the message becomes the title.
- All ticket texts are localized in English, German, Polish and Chinese. The primary button no longer renders in all caps.

- Das Ticket-Formular nutzt die ruhige Fläche, gedämpfte Labels und eine gefüllte Aktion wie der Rest der App.

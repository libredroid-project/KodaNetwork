2. Information We Collect & Process

KodaHosting processes certain data to function correctly:

- Account Information: When you register or log in, we securely process your email address and authentication details to manage your personal KodaHosting account. Authentication is handled securely by our partner, Supabase. We do not process your password directly. Supabase handles password storage and verification using industry-standard hashing algorithms.

- Web Dashboard & Remote Control: When using the Web Dashboard, we process your authentication tokens and proxy server commands securely via our API to allow remote management of your Android-hosted servers. This includes server start, stop, restart, hibernate, wipe, and delete commands, as well as property changes (RAM, MOTD, gamemode, difficulty, max players, PvP, whitelist) and console commands.

- Local Data: Your Minecraft server files, worlds, plugins, and configurations are stored purely locally on your device. We never access, transmit, or store this data on our servers. The developer cannot access, view, or recover your server files under any circumstances.

- Biometric Data: If you enable Biometric Security in the app settings, your device's fingerprint or face scan is used. This data never leaves your device and is processed securely by Android's local biometric system. We have no access to biometric data and cannot store or transmit it.

- Device Information: We check your device architecture (e.g., ARM64) and Android version locally to download the correct Java runtime environments and binary files. This information is not transmitted to us.

- IP Privacy: Your personal home IP address is never leaked. All multiplayer traffic is routed through our central VPS infrastructure. Players connecting to your server will only ever see the IP address of our secure proxy. We do not log player IP addresses beyond what is technically necessary for the operation of the proxy tunnel.

- AI Server Builder: If you use the AI Server Builder, your text prompts are sent to the Google Gemini API for processing to generate server configurations. Do not submit sensitive personal information in these prompts. Data transferred outside the European Economic Area (EEA) to Google LLC is safeguarded under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (DPF) and Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not store your prompts or the generated configurations on our servers.

- AI Crash Analysis ("Ask AI", opt-in): If you explicitly consent and tap "Ask AI" after a crash, the last portion of your app log (up to approximately 1,000 lines), basic device information (model, RAM, Android version), and the affected server's configuration are transmitted to OpenRouter, a U.S.-based AI gateway, for analysis. OpenRouter routes the request through third-party AI models, which may include models by NVIDIA (Nemotron), MiniMax, Dots Studio, inclusionAI (Ling), and Google (Gemma). The feature is limited to 10 requests per day, requires explicit consent before every first use, and can be revoked at any time in the app settings. Do not use this feature if your logs may contain sensitive personal information. Transfers to the U.S. are safeguarded under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (DPF) and Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable to the respective model provider.

- Support Tickets: If you contact us through the in-app support ticket system, the developer console, or request a password reset or verification code, we process your email address and your message, including diagnostic information attached to the ticket (device model, app version, memory, network type, and the names and status of your hosted servers). Emails are delivered via Resend, a third-party email delivery service.

- Moderation, Bans and Appeals: If we restrict or ban a device or account, we process the app/device identifier (app UUID and hardware ID), the reason, the time, the appeal messages you send and the decision. This data is used to enforce our terms, prevent abuse and handle appeals. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (security and abuse prevention). A rejected appeal starts a 7-day waiting period before another appeal can be opened. For severe abuse we can close ticket creation for an account completely; crisis support remains reachable in that case.

- Developer Console: If you publish extensions, you sign in to the web developer console with your KodaHosting account. We process your email address, verification status, extension metadata and packages, release versions, store listings, review notes, and developer support tickets. The console is hosted on the same infrastructure and uses the same account.

- Extension Download Statistics (first-party): When an extension is downloaded from the in-app store, we store the extension id, version, time, the device model, Android version and app build sent by the app, and whether the download came from the app or the website. This record contains no account id, device id, advertising id or IP address and is not linked to your account. Developers see only aggregated counts for their own extensions (for example which device models or app builds download them).

- Voluntary Donations: If you choose to support development via Ko-fi, you will be redirected to ko-fi.com, which processes the donation independently. Ko-fi may collect your name, email, and payment information. We do not process or store your payment data. Ko-fi's privacy policy applies to donations. Donations are voluntary gifts and do not create a customer relationship with us.

The App utilizes certain third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or provide core functionalities:

Where a provider processes data on our behalf (for example Supabase for storage and authentication, or the AI providers for crash analysis), it acts as our processor under Art. 28 GDPR and is bound by a data processing agreement. Where a provider decides on its own about the processing (for example when a payment or donation page is opened), it is a separate controller for that part and its own privacy policy applies. We recommend reviewing their respective privacy policies.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account information (email address and authentication data) for as long as your account is active. Server management metadata (server names, configurations, status) is stored in the Supabase database. When you delete a server or your account, the active entry is marked as deleted and removed from public listings; the server record itself is kept as a contract record for the statutory retention period described below. Your server files, worlds and plugins always stay on your device and are never uploaded to us.

Upon account deletion, your profile data (email address, Minecraft link, nickname, two-factor data and device tokens) is removed from our systems within 30 days, except where we are legally required to retain it or where it is stored in backups that are deleted on the normal backup cycle. Server records are retained for the contract retention period, and support tickets (including ban appeals) are retained as security and abuse records in anonymized form.

The following retention rules apply in addition:

- Extension download statistics are stored for 12 months and then deleted automatically by a daily cleanup job. They are not linked to an account.
- If you request a full data deletion and it is approved, your account and all personal data that is not legally required are deleted immediately. Records we must retain are moved into a separate, access-restricted retention table and deleted when the legal deadline ends: billing and payment records after 10 years (§ 147 AO, § 257 HGB), contract and server records after 6 years, and security or abuse evidence after 180 days.
- Data export requests create a ZIP in our storage. The download link is valid for seven days; after that you can request a new export. The export contains the data stored for your account and linked devices, including extension publishing data if you used the developer console.
- Support tickets, including developer support tickets and ban appeals, are kept while the ticket is open and afterwards as security and abuse records so decisions stay reviewable. When an account is deleted, the reference to the account is removed (the ticket is anonymized); the ticket itself is not deleted. The retention summary for security and abuse evidence is deleted after 180 days.
- Published extension packages are retained for 365 days. Only the newest approved package is publicly downloadable; support may hand out an older release inside this window for a documented reason, for example a legal request. After 365 days older package files are deleted, while the newest approved package stays available.

7. Your GDPR Rights (User Rights)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable privacy laws, you have the following rights:

- Right to Access (Art. 15 GDPR): You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- Right to Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR): You may update or correct your account information.
- Right to Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR): You can request the permanent deletion of your account and associated email address at any time.
- Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18 GDPR): You may request that we restrict the processing of your personal data under certain conditions.
- Right to Data Portability (Art. 20 GDPR): You can request a machine-readable copy of your personal data.
- Right to Object (Art. 21 GDPR): You may object to the processing of your personal data based on legitimate interests.
- Right to Withdraw Consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR): You may withdraw any consent you have given at any time.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at support@kodaserv.eu. If you are under 16, your request must be submitted by or with the consent of your legal guardian.

You can also use the account screen in the app: "Request my data" creates a data export after an admin review, "Request full data deletion" starts a deletion request that must be approved by an admin, and "My requests" shows the status. The developer console offers the same account and support paths for extension developers.

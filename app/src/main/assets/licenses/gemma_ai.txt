AI Crash Analysis via OpenRouter
================================

Feature: "Ask AI" crash analysis (user-initiated, opt-in consent,
max 10 requests/day).

Model chain: requests are routed by OpenRouter (https://openrouter.ai),
a U.S.-based AI gateway, through varying third-party models, which may
include:
- NVIDIA Nemotron (build.nvidia.com)
- MiniMax (minimax.io)
- Dots Studio / inclusionAI (Ling models)
- Google Gemma (https://ai.google.dev/gemma/terms)

Each model is provided by its respective vendor under its own terms of
use. See OpenRouter's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy on their
website for details on routing and retention.

Data notice: when the user taps "Ask AI", the last app log lines (up to
~1,000 lines), basic device information (model, RAM, Android version)
and the affected server's configuration are sent to OpenRouter for
analysis. Nothing is sent automatically; the feature requires explicit
consent before first use and can be revoked in the app settings.
See the app's Privacy Policy for details.

The AI response is an automated suggestion, NOT guaranteed-correct
support. The user is responsible for reviewing and applying any change.
